      Run of show
      45 minutes, once a week, covering the calendar week that ended Sunday.
      
      
      The call exists to make decisions. Numbers are read before it, updates are written before it, and anything on target is skipped. If the scorecard is clean and the issues list is short, end early.

        Rules of the call
        
          
          
            
        • No status updates. If it can be written, it is written before the call.
          • 
            
        • On target means skipped. Only missed or wrong-way rows get airtime, 60 seconds each: cause, next step.
          • 
            
        • Every issue ends in one of three things. A task with an owner and a date, a logged decision, or a deliberate park.
          • 
            
        • One owner per number and per task. Two names means nobody.
          • 
            
        • Ad-by-ad review stays out. That belongs in the Friday creative post-mortem. Only its conclusions come here.
          •

          • 
          
        • Chair. Pin the three issues that matter most.

        • Tasks with a ClickUp home get pushed there the same day.
          • 
          
        • Decisions stay in the log here so they are not re-argued.
          • 
          
        • Targets that changed are updated on the scorecard.

        What the call covers
        
      
        
    
        
  
        

  
        
    
        
      
        Definitions
        What each number means, where it comes from and when it becomes an issue.
        
      
        Three layers keep the call short. The weekly scorecard is the only thing read every week. Diagnostics, such as hook rate or cost per click, are opened only when a scorecard row is off. Slow numbers, such as 90-day value and payback, are reviewed in the first call of each month.

        The page is updated automatically once a week. Anything the update could not pull is listed here.

        Scorecard definitions
        
      
        
    
        
    
        
      
        How a row gets its status
        
      
          
        
        • With a target. On target, within 10% of target, or missed. Set targets in the Target column of the scorecard.
          • 
        
        • Without a target. A row is listed for discussion when it moves against us by 10% or more on last week, or sits 20% or more on the losing side of its median over the previous eight weeks.
          • 
        
        • Two misses in a row. The row becomes an issue on the Board with its owner's name on it.
